# S2 — iCalendar method: `REQUEST` vs `PUBLISH`

- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Question:** Which `.ics` method adds, updates in place and removes events without Gmail RSVP buttons competing with TapNShow's own buttons? (spec §9, §13)
- **Result:** **`PUBLISH` rejected; `REQUEST` with a pre-accepted attendee, sent after the member responds** (owner decision 2026-10-05).

## Setup

- Sender: a Gmail account over SMTP (nodemailer `icalEvent`, MIME `text/calendar; method=…` alternative part + `invite.ics` attachment; structure verified by rendering locally).
- Recipient: a **different** Gmail account (Gmail web + Google Calendar). Outlook.com and Apple Calendar were **not tested** (owner decision); revisit before M4 ships.
- Steps per UID: invite (SEQUENCE 0, 18:00) → update (SEQUENCE 1, +1 h) → cancel (`METHOD:CANCEL`, `STATUS:CANCELLED`, SEQUENCE 2).
- Variants: `REQUEST` (attendee `RSVP=TRUE`), `PUBLISH`, and two "quiet" `REQUEST`s: `RSVP=FALSE` + `PARTSTAT=NEEDS-ACTION` (quiet-a) and `RSVP=FALSE` + `PARTSTAT=ACCEPTED` (quiet-b).

## Observations (recipient ≠ sender)

| | `PUBLISH` | `REQUEST` | quiet-a | quiet-b (pre-accepted) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Event card in Gmail | Yes, "Add to Calendar" | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Gmail Yes/No/Maybe buttons | No | **Yes** | **Yes** | No — shows "Yes ▾" already selected |
| Auto-added to calendar | Not on first contact | Not on first contact | Yes* | Yes* |
| Update moves event in place | **No — duplicate created**, old one kept | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Cancel removes event | **No** — card says "Removed" but both copies stayed | Yes | Yes | Yes |

\* The recipient's Google Calendar setting is **"Add invitations to my calendar: Only if the sender is known"**. The first `REQUEST` was not auto-added; after the recipient replied "Yes" once, later invites from the same sender were. Auto-add therefore depends on each member's setting and prior contact, not on the variant.

A same-account test (sender = recipient) showed no card at all — Gmail treats self-sent invites as the organizer's own; invalid as evidence.

## Decision

- `PUBLISH` cannot update or cancel reliably → rejected (would break spec §7.5).
- The first invite email carries **only TapNShow's Attend / Late / Absent buttons — no `.ics`** (no competing Gmail RSVP).
- When a member answers Attend or Late, a **calendar confirmation email** is sent with `METHOD:REQUEST`, the member as attendee with `RSVP=FALSE` and `PARTSTAT=ACCEPTED`, a stable `UID`, and `SEQUENCE` = meeting `ics_sequence`.
- Meeting edits send `REQUEST` with `SEQUENCE+1` to members who have a calendar copy; switching to Absent or cancelling the meeting sends `METHOD:CANCEL` to them.
- Cost: one extra email per attending member (counts against the organizer's Gmail quota).
- **Verify in M4:** behavior of a pre-accepted invite from a sender the member does not "know" yet (first contact) under "Only if the sender is known"; Outlook and Apple Calendar behavior.
- Cosmetic: include the attendee's name (spike showed "Unnamed attendee").
